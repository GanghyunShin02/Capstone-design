Motivation

  • We study refrence cell at Ch.1 and Ch.2

  • In this chapter we study cell’s connect, mesh.

Mesh Entities

  • A mesh is built from topological entities of difference dimensions

  • Call entities as Cell’s vertices,edges,cell all.

Topological entitie pair \((d,i)\)
\(d\) is dimention \(i\) is index ; unique number at that dimenstion.

Adjacency

  • Adjacency is entitie’s connection.

\[ Adj_{d_1,d_2}(n)=(1,2,3) \]

\(d_1\) dimention n’s entitie cell has \(d_2\) dimention’s entitie number 1,2,3. n’s triangle origined 1,2,3 entities.

Mesh Geometry

  • We know cell’s connect by adjacency.
  • In mesh geometry, we know positon of cell.

Convert refrence cell to physical cell by Jacobian.

\[ (\zeta,\eta) \rightarrow (x,y) \]

\[ J=\begin{pmatrix} \frac{\partial \zeta}{\partial x} & \frac{\partial \zeta}{\partial y} \\ \frac{\partial \eta}{\partial x} & \frac{\partial \eta}{\partial y} \end{pmatrix} \]

  • dolfinx calculate jacobian auto.

  • How calculate Jacobian at first lagrange space

  • shape function

\[ x(\zeta,\eta)=(1-\zeta-\eta)x_0+\zeta x_1+\eta x_2 \]

\[ y(\zeta,\eta)=(1-\zeta-\eta)y_0+\zeta x_1+\eta y_2 \]

\[ J=\begin{pmatrix} \frac{\partial \zeta}{\partial x} & \frac{\partial \zeta}{\partial y} \\ \frac{\partial \eta}{\partial x} & \frac{\partial \eta}{\partial y} \end{pmatrix} \]

\[ J=\begin{pmatrix} -x_0+x_1 & -x_0+x_2 \\ -y_0+y_1 & -y_0+y_2 \end{pmatrix} \]

Exercise 2 — Adjacency

  1. Create a \(3 \times 3\) unit square mesh
  2. Compute and print \(\mathrm{Adj}_{2,0}\), \(\mathrm{Adj}_{2,1}\), and \(\mathrm{Adj}_{1,0}\) for all entities
  3. For edge 0, find which cells contain it (upward adjacency \(\mathrm{Adj}_{1,2}\))
  4. Verify: each interior edge is shared by exactly 2 cells, each boundary edge by 1
